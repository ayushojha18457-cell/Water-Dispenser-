# 💧 Simple Water Dispenser

A simple, low-cost, DIY water dispenser project designed to demonstrate
the basic working of a small water pump using a battery and a switch.

This project is suitable for students, beginners, and school science
exhibitions. It demonstrates the basic principles of electrical circuits
and water pumping.

## 📸 Project Overview

The Simple Water Dispenser is a small prototype made using a mini water
pump, a battery, connecting wires, and a switch.

When the switch is turned on, electrical power is supplied to the pump.
The pump then moves water from a container through a suitable tube.
When the switch is turned off, the pump stops.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

The dispenser works using electrical energy from a battery.

1. The battery supplies electrical power to the circuit.
2. The switch controls the power supplied to the pump.
3. When the switch is ON, the pump operates.
4. The pump moves water through the tube.
5. When the switch is OFF, the pump stops.

This project demonstrates the conversion of electrical energy into
mechanical energy to move water.

## 🔌 Basic Circuit Concept

Battery (+) → Switch → Pump (+)

Battery (-) → Pump (-)

The pump's polarity and wiring should follow its manufacturer's
instructions. Check all connections before operating the project.

## 🛠️ Construction Steps

1. Collect all the required components.
2. Check the battery voltage and pump specifications.
3. Connect the battery, switch, and pump using suitable wires.
4. Secure the electrical connections and insulate exposed conductors.
5. Connect a suitable tube to the pump's water outlet.
6. Place the pump in the appropriate water container according to
   its design and instructions.
7. Test the switch and check whether water flows through the tube.
8. Make sure the electrical parts remain dry during operation.

## ⚠️ Safety Precautions

- Keep the battery, switch, and exposed electrical connections dry.
- Do not immerse a pump unless it is specifically designed for
  submerged operation.
- Never connect a lithium-ion battery directly to a charger without
  appropriate charging and protection circuitry.
- Avoid short circuits and damaged wires.
- Disconnect the battery before changing the wiring.
- Do not use water from this prototype for drinking unless every
  water-contact component is food-safe and the complete system has
  been made hygienic and suitable for drinking water.
